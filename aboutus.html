About Emmanuel

Farmer, founder, and DDI trainer

Emmanuel Karisa Baya is a farmer and the founder of Magarini Children Centre and Organic Demonstration Farm. He received his Diploma from the Deep Democracy Institute of Africa in May 2021 and is the first Kenyan DDI Diplomat.

  • DDI Diploma
  • First Kenyan Diplomat
  • International Trainer

His diploma thesis is titled “Peace, conflict and politics from the soil.” He is now an international trainer for the Deep Democracy Institute.
