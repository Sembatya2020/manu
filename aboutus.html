About

A son of Magarini, trained across the world, working at home

I am Emmanuel Karisa Baya: a Processwork Diplomate, facilitator, teacher, community builder and Member of County Assembly for Marafa Ward in Kilifi County, Kenya. For 25 years my work has had one purpose: helping people be heard, so that they can shape their own future together.

My story

From Kaembeni to the County Assembly

Where I come from

I was born in Kaembeni, Bomani, in Magarini, and grew up among farming families on Kenya's coast. I went to Kaembeni Primary School and Marafa Secondary School. I first trained in accounting in Nairobi, but I soon found that my real calling was working with people and communities.

Learning from the community

In 2001 I began as a volunteer community mobiliser with the Institute of Participatory Development in Malindi. Over the next decade I worked with ActionAid International Kenya, World Vision Kenya, the Kenya National Federation of Agricultural Producers and the Women Enterprise Fund. I organised farmers, trained community facilitators, supported women's enterprise, and served as a civic educator during Kenya's 2010 constitutional review.

Building what the community needed

I helped farmers in Langobaya form an irrigation association that grew into the biggest irrigation scheme in Malindi District, and I set up the first KENFAP branch in Malindi District. In 2008 I founded the Magarini Children Centre and Organic Farming Demonstration Farm to care for orphaned and vulnerable children and to teach organic farming.

Searching for better ways to lead

My work kept bringing me face to face with conflict: between families, clans, groups and leaders. I wanted better tools to help people through it. That search took me to Japan, Thailand, the United States, Canada, Poland, Germany, Ukraine and Egypt, where I studied rural leadership, peace and conflict resolution, Worldwork and Deep Democracy. In 2021 I graduated as a Processwork Diplomate through the Deep Democracy Institute, and I co-founded Deep Democracy Kenya to bring this work home.

Serving my people

After contesting in 2013 and 2017, I was elected Member of County Assembly for Marafa Ward in 2022. Today I bring together everything I have learned: listening to every voice, facilitating difficult conversations, and turning community needs into action.

What I believe

  • Every voice matters.

    The quiet, unpopular or unheard voice often holds what a group needs most.

  • Conflict carries information.

    Handled with awareness, it shows a community what needs to change.

  • Leadership is service.

    Good leaders live alongside the people they serve and grow leadership in others.

  • Change starts at home.

    Lasting solutions come from within communities, not only from outside.

Since 2022

Serving Marafa Ward

As Member of County Assembly for Marafa Ward, I represent the people of Marafa in the Kilifi County Assembly. My work includes:

  • Policy formulation: shaping county laws and policies that affect Marafa residents
  • Project planning and implementation: making sure county projects reflect what the community actually needs
  • Oversight: following up on how projects are delivered and how public money is used
  • Engaging the county government: raising the needs of the ward directly, including health and safety at Marafa Level 4 Hospital

My facilitation training shapes how I lead. Before decisions are made, I listen to every side, including the quiet ones.

Healing History

I work with an international peace group on Healing History, alongside Gary and Sage, and Brad and Amanda. Together we facilitate processes that address the historical wounds of colonialism, conflict and injustice that still shape how communities relate to one another today.
