Hon. Emmanuel Karisa Baya

Processwork Diplomate, facilitator, teacher and Member of County Assembly for Marafa Ward.

For 25 years I have worked alongside the communities of Kenya's coast as a mobiliser, founder, facilitator and elected leader. Today I teach and facilitate in Kenya and online, helping groups, organisations and leaders work through conflict and build a future together.
