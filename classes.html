Track 1

Processwork and psychology

Understand what moves people, groups and communities beneath the surface.

  • Processwork-Oriented Psychology

    Online

    An introduction to Processwork, the approach developed by Arnold Mindell. You will learn to follow dreams, body signals, relationship patterns and conflict as sources of meaning and change, rather than problems to get rid of.

    For: counsellors, coaches, teachers, social workers and anyone curious about inner and group life.

    You will gain: a new way of noticing what is trying to happen in yourself and others.

    Register

  • Deep Democracy in Practice

    Online

    Deep Democracy means every voice, feeling and viewpoint has something important to contribute, including the unpopular ones. This class shows how to bring minority voices into decisions so that agreements last.

    For: committees, boards, faith groups, organisations and community leaders.

    You will gain: practical tools for decisions that people actually support.

    Register

  • Healing History

    Online or in person

    Communities carry wounds from colonialism, conflict and injustice that still shape how people relate today. Drawing on current work with an international peace group, this class explores how groups can face that history together and begin to heal.

    For: peacebuilders, community leaders, educators and anyone working across divides.

    You will gain: ways to open difficult historical conversations safely and with respect.

    Register

Track 2

Leadership and facilitation

Lead and facilitate with awareness, especially when things get difficult.

  • Leadership

    Online

    Leadership is not only a position. This class looks at leading through tension, using your rank and power well, and growing leadership in the people around you.

    For: new and experienced leaders in communities, organisations and public office.

    You will gain: confidence to lead when the room is divided.

    Register

  • Large Group Facilitation

    Online or in person

    How to facilitate community dialogues, public forums and heated group processes where many voices and strong feelings are present. Based on Worldwork methods and years of public participation work in Kilifi County.

    For: county officers, NGO staff, community mobilisers and trainers.

    You will gain: the skills to hold a large, emotional group and help it move forward.

    Register

  • Small Group Facilitation

    Online or in person

    How to facilitate teams, boards, committees and families, including when there is conflict or silence in the room.

    For: managers, team leaders, chairpersons and group facilitators.

    You will gain: tools to turn tense meetings into honest and productive ones.

    Register

  • Conflict Resolution and Worldwork

    Online

    Worldwork applies Processwork to conflict between groups, communities and nations. Drawing on training in the USA, Poland and Egypt, this class shows how to work with conflict instead of avoiding it.

    For: peacebuilders, mediators, community leaders and human rights workers.

    You will gain: a clear method for facilitating conflict between groups.

    Register

  • Conscious Use of Power and Rank

    Online

    Everyone holds some kind of power: social, structural, psychological or spiritual. Based on Inner Activist training in Canada, this class helps you see your own rank and use it to serve others rather than silence them.

    For: leaders, officials, managers and activists.

    You will gain: awareness of how power shapes every relationship you lead.

    Register

  • Peacebuilding for Community Leaders

    Online or in person

    Practical peace and dialogue skills for people who hold their communities together, drawn from peace and conflict studies at Chulalongkorn University in Thailand.

    For: village elders, religious leaders, youth leaders and local officials.

    You will gain: simple, tested ways to prevent and respond to community conflict.

    Register

Track 3

Community development

Build organisations and communities that last.

  • Community Building

    Online

    How to bring every voice into the room, including the ones usually left out, and turn division into shared direction.

    For: community organisers, CBO members, church and youth groups.

    You will gain: ways to build trust and a shared vision in your community.

    Register

  • Community Mobilization and Organising

    Online or in person

    How to start, register and run a community-based organisation or farmers' association, drawn from founding the Magarini Children Centre and the Langobaya Irrigation Farmers Association.

    For: people starting or leading CBOs, self-help groups and associations.

    You will gain: a step-by-step path from an idea to a working organisation.

    Register

  • Resource Mobilization

    Online

    How community organisations find funding, partners and local resources, and keep them.

    For: CBO and NGO leaders, project officers and volunteers.

    You will gain: a practical plan for funding your work beyond a single donor.

    Register

  • Civic Education and Public Participation

    In person

    How citizens can engage county government in planning, budgets and project oversight, taught by a serving Member of County Assembly and former civic educator.

    For: residents, ward committees, youth and women's groups, and civil society.

    You will gain: the knowledge to make your voice count in county decisions.

    Register

  • Organic Farming and Sustainable Agriculture

    In person, Marafa

    Hands-on training at the Magarini Organic Farming Demonstration Farm, backed by rural leadership training at the Asian Rural Institute in Japan.

    For: farmers, farmer groups, schools and agricultural students.

    You will gain: practical organic methods you can use on your own land.

    Register

  • Rural Leadership

    Online or in person

    Servant leadership for village and community leaders, rooted in the Asian Rural Institute's approach of leading by living alongside the people you serve.

    For: community leaders in rural areas.

    You will gain: a leadership style built on trust, humility and shared work.

    Register
